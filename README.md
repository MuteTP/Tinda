# Tinda

**A free, offline point-of-sale app for cafés, milk tea shops and snack stands.**

Tinda turns your Android phone or tablet into a simple cash register. Ring up orders, take
cash or e-wallet payments, apply Senior Citizen and PWD discounts, share receipts and see
how your shop is doing, all without an internet connection.

No account. No subscription. No internet needed. Your sales stay on your device.

## Install

1. Download the APK from the link above.
2. Open the downloaded file. You can find it in your notification bar or in the
   **Downloads** folder.
3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**, then
   go back and tap **Install**.
4. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. This
   happens because Tinda is installed directly instead of through the Play Store.
5. Open **Tinda** from your home screen.

**To update**, download the newer APK and install it over the old one. Your menu and
sales are kept.

## What you can do

- **Sell fast**: tap products to add them to the order, and search or filter by category.
- **Take payment**: cash (with change calculation), GCash, Maya, card or other.
- **Give discounts**: Senior Citizen and PWD (20%), or other discounts of 10%, 5% or a
  custom amount.
- **Share receipts** through SMS, Messenger, Viber or email.
- **Track sales**: see every receipt by day, and void a sale if you made a mistake.
- **See reports**: totals, best sellers and sales charts for any period, with export to
  CSV for Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Make it yours**: add your shop name, logo, address and receipt message, and pick your
  shop's colors.
- **Back up your data** to Google Drive, Files or anywhere you like.

Works on phones and tablets. On a tablet, the order appears beside the menu.

## How to use

### 1. Set up your menu

The first time you open Tinda, choose one of these:

- **Use a sample menu**: start with a ready-made Café, Milk Tea or Snacks menu and edit
  it.
- **Add my own products**: start from an empty menu.

Go to the **Menu** tab to add, edit or hide products and to manage categories. Each
product can have its own tile color. Turn off **Show this product on the Sell screen** to
hide an item that's sold out.

### 2. Set up your shop

Go to the **Settings** tab and fill in:

- **Shop name**, **Address / contact** and **Receipt message**. These print on every
  receipt.
- **Shop logo**: replaces the Tinda logo on the Sell screen and appears on receipts.
- **Currency symbol**
- **Appearance**: your brand color, background, light or dark mode, and the striped
  awning.

### 3. Make a sale

1. On the **Sell** tab, tap a product to add it to the order. To enter a quantity,
   press and hold the product.
2. Tap **Checkout**.
3. To apply a discount, tap **Discount**:
   - **Senior Citizen / PWD**: enter the OSCA or PWD ID number and, if you like, the
     name. For group orders, pick only the items the senior or PWD will consume under
     **Items covered**.
   - **Other discount**: choose 10%, 5% or a custom amount, and add a note saying why.
   - Only one discount can be used per sale.
4. Choose how the customer is paying. For cash, tap a quick-amount button or enter the
   amount received, and Tinda shows the **change due**.
5. Tap **Charge**. The receipt appears. Tap **Share** to send it to the customer, or
   **New sale** to take the next order.

### 4. Check your sales

- **Sales** tab: every receipt, grouped by day. Tap a sale to see its receipt.
  If a sale was a mistake, tap **Void sale**. Voided sales stay on record but are not
  counted in your totals, and you can restore them later.
- **Reports** tab: pick today, yesterday, the last 7 days, this month, last month or a
  custom range. You'll see total sales, discounts, number of transactions, average sale,
  items sold, a sales chart, and your top products, categories and payment methods. Tap
  **CSV** to export the report.

### 5. Back up your data

> **Important:** Tinda keeps everything on your device. If you uninstall the app, clear its
> storage or lose your phone, your sales are gone unless you have a backup.

- **Back up**: go to **Settings → Back up data** and save the file to Google Drive, Files,
  Messenger or anywhere safe. Do this regularly, for example at the end of each day or
  week.
- **Restore**: go to **Settings → Restore from backup** and pick your backup file. Use this
  to move your shop to a new phone. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app.

## Requirements

- An Android phone or tablet running Android 5.0 or newer
- The download is about 25 MB

## Privacy

Tinda doesn't collect, upload or share any of your data. It has no ads, no tracking and no
account sign-up. Your data only leaves the device when you share a receipt, export a
report or save a backup.
